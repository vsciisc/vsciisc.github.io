Notes for class on 13/1/2026
  • Education is shift of body to mind. Body needs are nothing but animal extincts.
  • Character is formed by repeated thoughts, actions, and habits, we become what we consistently think and do.
  • Suffering and struggle teaches more than comfort, awakening inner strength and wisdom.
  • True power lies in inner personality and will, not merely words, intellect, or outward achievements. Personality is the reason why certain people though having less degrees can influence more people than a person with all the degrees.

  • Real education is man making, transmitted through character, example, love, and inner transformation rather than information alone.
