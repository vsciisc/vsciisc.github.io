Notes for class on 17/3/2026
  • In the last class we completed the book His call to the Nations.
  • As a concluding lecture there were multiple important comments made by Swamiji in terms of keeping up the ideals with respect to India.
  • Renunciation and service are the national ideals of India.
  • One must have faith in oneself that he is born to do great things.
  • India will be raised with the power of spirit, and not with the power of soul.
  • If one is able to sacrifice his/her life for a cause then only he/she can become a leader.

  • Be the servant while leading.
  • Swamiji's faith is on the young generation who are lions.
  • True equality can never be achieved on earth.
  • God and Truth are the only politics in the world.
